Welcome to Our School

The school was established in 1856 and sifted to the present new building with total land area about 5.2 are in the year 2003. It is located in Jahajpul area of Hisar city. The school has well designed building with very good infrastructural facilities. There are approximately 30 class rooms and 20 other rooms. There is a well established Library in the school. There is about 1.5 acre land for playground. The school has well equipped Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Geography and computer laboratories.

All the staff works very hard to make the best possible efforts to inculcate moral and ethical values intertwined with academic as well as extracurricular activities in the students. Our School is a student-centered educational institute delivering excellence in education. This school team is committed to students, communities, and each other. This commitment to students and dedication to impacting their value education through innovative methods makes us unique. We prepare students to become lifelong learners and responsible citizens ready to meet the challenges of the future in partnership with families and community.

Our Mission

Education is not preparation for life but education is life itself and the one who develops a passion for learning, will never be ceased to grow. Our goal is to support and nurture the children's and our own natural desire to be life-long learners. We are committed to the families we serve, providing support and encouragement. Our Mission is to provide high quality education and childcare in a safe, respectful and inclusive environment that builds a foundation for life-long learning.

Our Vision

Vision of the school is to develop confident and responsible individuals who aspire to achieve their full potential. We will achieve this by providing a welcoming, happy, safe, and supportive learning environment in which all students are equal and all achievements are celebrated. We believe that a happy child is a successful one. We provide a healthy environment to develop students physically as well as mentally.
